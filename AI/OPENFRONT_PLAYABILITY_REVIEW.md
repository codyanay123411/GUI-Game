# OpenFront playability comparison and proposed direction

Planning report, 2026-10-11. **Investigation only; no implementation or Director acceptance implied.** Local project evidence includes the uncommitted admin audit. OpenFront references are pinned to `a15138358930c10241e9ceb4be1ce3389477e970`.

## Recommendation and five highest-impact findings

**Choose A: retain province-based mechanics and improve decision clarity first.** The current game already has contextual actions, conquest, commitment, attention markers, relationship workflows, communications, public player comparison, presentation settings and final-map inspection. Replacing the territorial model would replace accepted contracts before we know whether presentation is the main obstacle. OpenFront's useful lesson is connecting the next decision to the relevant location, legal action and visible consequence.

1. **Legal targets are difficult to recognize.** The map has no legal-target overlay or ownership legend. Unavailable Conquer/War actions disappear; their explanation is inside Province info. The authored map allows corner adjacency, which visual gaps between provinces do not explain. Show legal borders/targets and a short reason at selection, using existing rules and server revalidation.
2. **Commitment and battle presentation need a clearer sequence.** The normal conquest review starts at `0`, then declares and submits an amount, then opens Military for Ready. In this session, clicking Ready immediately reduced the troop total while the UI said “Battle in progress / Resolving in 1s.” Distinguish accepted reservation, locked forces, mechanical resolution and ownership/result presentation. Preserve the existing timing and privacy rules.
3. **Attention exists but does not reliably locate distant activity.** Off-screen markers are hidden; selecting an event does not pan the camera. Improve the existing attention system with locate/focus and an unobtrusive off-screen indication, rather than adding another permanent notification panel.
4. **Touch readiness is not established.** Responsive buttons and a compact radial fallback exist, but map pan/select handlers specifically accept mouse input. There is no inspected touch pan/pinch equivalent or explicit recenter control. Treat touch navigation as a separate verification and implementation milestone, not a desktop-layout pass.
5. **The objective and feudal progression need explanation.** The persistent hint says “Domination: 12 territories,” without the player's current realm count. After a successful ordinary conquest, Titles showed realm control `2/2`, Test Prestige `0/100`, and disabled Establish Duchy. Prestige earning is not implemented; presentation alone cannot make that progression playable. Explain the prototype limit now; any real Prestige economy requires a Director decision.

These are playability hypotheses, not evidence that a redesign will improve retention. No novice usability or full human match was observed.

### Desktop and touch evidence

Current native observations used Studio's **HD720 (nominal 1280×720)** and **VGA (nominal 640×480)** desktop profiles. The rendered emulator is scaled within Studio; actual ScreenGui dimensions were not newly measured. Previous cleanup evidence recorded 1280×662 and 640×422 drawable sizes, plus a 1920-wide profile; those remain historical measurements, not newly observed values.

HD720: selected labels, Info/Conquer buttons, review, Ready and result were readable. Context review hid the right-hand provinces; transition to Military changed the map's fitted extent. Selecting Greyfen while the Westmere radial was open worked through non-button overlay space, supporting the existing click-through behavior. This was not a complete current drag/hover/layering regression test.

VGA: Military and Titles drawer text/buttons remained usable; many province labels disappeared as the map narrowed. The bottom hint clipped after “Drag to pan.” Communications displayed channel/audience text with a scrollbar; composing/delivery was not tested. This is a readability issue even if panel bounds pass.

**Touch:** no phone/tablet profile or physical touch device was exercised. Mouse pan/select source branches reject non-MouseButton1 input and drag listens for MouseMovement; no touch/pinch equivalent was found in that controller. GUI Activated buttons and compact radial layout are useful groundwork but do not establish playable map navigation. Portrait 390×844, landscape 844×390 and tablet 1024×768 are proposed test sizes, not passes or current screenshots. Measure tap targets in actual logical pixels, safe areas, scrolling, pan-versus-select cancellation, pinch and radial placement. Do not borrow OpenFront's long-press behavior without deciding whether discoverability warrants it here.

## Proposed map states and contextual behavior

All proposals use existing authorized/public projections; eligibility is a UX prediction and the server remains authoritative. Use symbols/patterns and short labels alongside color. Maintain selected outline, animation/transparency and non-button click-through; avoid an opaque full-area hitbox.

| Selected context | Information and next actions | Guard / unresolved choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Own personal province | Personal owner/root, own army/economy relevance, Military recruitment and applicable battle; distinct selected border | Ownership does not authorize independent external war if the ruler is a subject. |
| Subject/same realm | Direct owner plus root/liege, realm contribution; Council when permitted; Direct only for personally adjacent rulers | Same-root membership alone never grants Direct. Root may wage external war through subject border; subject does not independently commit in root war. |
| Allied foreign realm | Ally badge and treaty effect, eligible alliance communications/diplomacy; War only if current rules allow | Alliance is not an NAP. Never imply shared troops, mandatory defense or shared victory. |
| Unclaimed | Public militia, legal border cue and Conquer/reason | Failed defense persists; no NPC ruler, Gold loot or invented regeneration. |
| Enemy / unrelated foreign ruler | Public owner/root/relationship, legal war reason, available permitted communication/relationship actions | Do not show private total/available troops, lock status or combat internals. Enemy relation does not override NAP/cooldown/root rules. |
| Contested / threatened | Authorized local contest marker, phase/deadline and locate/action; public unavailable reason where already permitted | No unrelated third-party war-intelligence overlay. How strongly threat cues should interrupt offers remains a usability decision. |
| Recently conquered | Brief ownership-change cue and permitted result summary; direct-owner/root colors update | Duration and optional result retention are presentation decisions; longer history requires explicit scope/privacy review. |
| Match ended | Final ownership and membership, result entry, inspection/Players/Messages; visible frozen status | No competitive controls, phantom progression prompt or implied rematch. |

Hover can cheaply preview public name/owner and selection affordance on desktop; touch needs an explicit tap state instead. A legal-target view should derive from the authored graph, including corner neighbors. A contested pattern should supplement, not overwrite, personal-owner/root meaning. Alternate overlays are optional: first test whether one coherent legend plus selected-target cues suffices.

## Geography and the three map directions

Vale's sixteen irregular quadrilateral provinces are a small authored grid with eight-neighbor adjacency. Geography already controls realm borders, personal Direct communication and legal expansion; hierarchy can create distant holdings. It lacks inspected terrain-based combat variation and the grid offers limited chokepoint variety. Those are model characteristics, not evidence that continuous conquest would be superior. No larger-map, high-player-count or simultaneous-event benchmark was run.

| Direction | Benefit / drawback | Contracts and cost | Performance/network implications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A: provinces, clearer presentation** | Makes legal decisions, ownership, phases and focus understandable; does not add tactical terrain or continuous fronts | Lowest relative cost: controllers/projections/GUI, preserve ownership, root graph, titles, treaties, personal troop ledger, persistent province militia and 12/16 victory. New authored topology/balance still requires approval. | Reuse snapshots; avoid expensive per-frame full-state rebuilds. More markers/labels require culling/grouping and measured larger-map UI budgets, but no territory-resolution expansion is necessary. |
| **B: political provinces + finer detail** | Non-mechanical terrain/art/zoom detail can improve orientation. Tactical subdivisions might add decisions but create two ownership meanings | Cosmetic-only B is moderate asset/render work and must clearly state no combat effect. Mechanical B is high cost: decide partial occupation, province control threshold, who owns militia, which borders establish diplomacy/Direct, troop reservation/casualty timing and title/victory counts. | Decorative detail can remain local. Tactical cells create additional state/deltas, UI reconciliation and simultaneous resolution; measure memory/render/network budgets rather than assuming visual detail is free. |
| **C: continuous tile/frontier conquest** | Visible incremental territorial pressure and terrain choice; harder to read stable feudal holdings and existing atomic outcomes | Highest cost; replacement design decisions for tile-to-ruler/province ownership, feudal control and root borders, treaty adjacency, titles' territory counts, ongoing troop losses/reservations/retreat, neutral defenders per area and domination denominator. Accepted war/commitment/result contracts would be superseded deliberately. | Many cells and frontier operations need compact representation, batching, deterministic authority/reconciliation, update-rate limits and stress testing. **A is the smallest coherent direction.** Cosmetic B may follow if orientation tests show geography is hard to remember; do not draw terrain implying bonuses that do not exist. Tactical B/C require new Director design work and evidence that the province loop remains unsatisfying after clarity improvements. OpenFront's tile simulation is not a drop-in renderer for our game.

## Simplicity and priorities

Always visible: own Gold/income, own available/committed troops, personal/root identity where ambiguous, current greater-realm domination progress, and a relevant urgent deadline. At selection: direct owner/root, relationship, authorized legal actions and short unavailable reason; public militia or the local player's battle context. Relevant-only: offers, lock/result cues, audience/recipient, attention counts. Secondary: detailed hierarchy, title requirements, economy breakdown, settings/help and public Players comparison. Preserve existing destinations; trim repeated explanatory text only when the decision remains understandable.

| Priority | Proposed bounded work | Completion evidence / authorization category |
| --- | --- | --- |

None authorized by this report. |
| Larger future work | Mechanical B/C and large-map/server scaling | Only after a model decision and measured province-loop limits; separate feasibility prototype and authoritative contracts. |

Preserve server-owned validation and service boundaries, bilateral hierarchy/diplomacy, personal resources, distinct Direct/Council/Allies/Diplomatic access, hidden military information, neutral-defense persistence, first-result freeze, inspection after defeat, and recoverable retired/admin assets. Keep debug state, seeded battles and creator-global settings separate from ordinary-player decisions. The admin cleanup and its owner/mock verification are not evidence of ordinary usability or multi-account security in this investigation.

Do not borrow OpenFront code, art, branding, exact layout, exposed enemy troop intelligence, donation/allied army assumptions, tile casualty formula, or control keys. Borrow the interaction principles only: relevant action at relevant location, legible commitment, reachable attention, and guidance responding to actual actions.

## Small measurable playtest

Use 6–8 first-time players, initially on the unchanged baseline, then the same tasks on the proposed P1 milestone. Separate desktop and genuine touch cohorts; counterbalance order where feasible. Use an independent root, direct subject and foreign opponent/NAP partner; include a corner-adjacent neutral target and an off-screen attack. No admin tools exposed to participants. Report small-sample descriptive results, not statistical retention claims.

| Measure | Task / recorded outcome | Proposed milestone criterion (not a current pass) |

| Orientation | Within 60s identify own land, personal versus realm control, available troops and victory objective | At least 80% correctly explain all four without coaching |
| Discoverability | Find a legal neutral target and explain a blocked target, including corner adjacency | At least 80% succeed within 30s; record mistaken geometry assumptions |
| Interaction success | Commit a chosen amount, Ready, recruit an affordable amount, send/decline a treaty or fealty offer | At least 90% valid task completion; count clicks/time/retries and server rejections |

### Existing authority and conflicts

[Game Design: Title Progression V0.1](GAME_DESIGN.md#title-progression-v01) and [Decisions](DECISIONS.md) lock sequential manual Lord → Duke → King → Emperor, server revalidation, map-configured ruler-plus-descendants territory counts, and Prestige **checked, not spent**. ### Recommended exact rule

**Start at 0 Prestige. Earn 10 Prestige per personally owned eligible province per minute of active match time.** Use one configurable map setting, proposed `prestigePerPersonalProvincePerMinute = 10` for Vale. No rank multiplier, minimum holding period, capture bonus, decay, spending, inactivity check, cap or carryover between matches in this first mechanic.

`earned delta = rate × integral(personal eligible province count over elapsed server seconds) / 60`.

Only direct ownership counts for earning. Subjects earn independently at the same rate; their land generates nothing for the liege. Accepting/releasing fealty changes title territory eligibility through the existing subtree contract, but neither grants Prestige nor changes a ruler's earning rate unless direct ownership actually changes. A subject's title territory count is its own subordinate subtree, not every holding under its ultimate root.

Normal starting allocation and ordinary validated conquest ownership are eligible. Earning starts when possession actually becomes authoritative; pending declarations/reservations and cosmetic combat presentation do not count as possession. Repeated captures merely divide a province's elapsed ownership intervals: no interval pays twice and no capture itself pays. Loss reduces the future rate immediately but preserves accumulated Prestige. Zero eligible holdings means zero earning; existing titles remain unchanged. Earning and establishment stop at the first authoritative match end; the final balance remains inspectable.

**Recommended interpretation of “no rewards for admin mutations”:** no instant grant AND no passive earning from administratively assigned holdings. Server-only ownership provenance must distinguish starting/gameplay acquisition from administrative assignment. An actual admin transfer marks the new holding ineligible until a later ordinary validated ownership transfer; a same-owner no-op does not reset an existing legitimate interval. The old owner's legitimately elapsed time is still settled, not penalized. Debug force-resolution/seeded ownership must also be classified as administrative, not laundered into an ordinary capture. Existing authorized `setprestige` remains an explicit test override, never an earned reward or ordinary progression claim. This stricter provenance interpretation needs approval; the weaker alternative (no event bonus, but future passive earning on admin land) is **not** this recommendation.

| Scenario | Duke: 2 land / 100 | King: 5 / 250 | Emperor: 10 / 500 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One personal province, no descendants | Prestige at 10 min; title blocked by land | Prestige at 25 min; title blocked | Prestige at 50 min; title blocked |

Expansion arithmetic: each two-minute interval contributes `20 × current holdings` Prestige. At minute 4 total is 60; another 40 takes 1 min 20 sec at 3 holdings. At minute 8 total is 200; another 50 takes 1 minute at 5. At minute 18 total is `20 × (1+…+9) = 900`; Emperor is land-gated, not Prestige-gated. ### Accounting and lifecycle

Keep fractional accumulation server-side; display whole Prestige rounded **down** and the current personal rate/next threshold. One province earns 1/6 Prestige per second at this rate. Never round each tick or ownership interval, and never round up to qualify; retain remainder across scheduling delays and transfers. Eligibility uses the authoritative accumulated value, not the displayed/client prediction. A scheduler can refresh roughly once per second; the formula uses elapsed server time, not “one reward per callback.” A rate change must settle the old rate before the new rate applies.

One guarded owner for the accumulator and one last-settled timestamp per ruler. Settle **previous** eligible counts to the ownership transition time, then replace counts from authoritative direct ownership. Process the actual revision once; duplicate notifications, Ready requests and repeated snapshots must not credit time again. Do not count recipients from greater-realm trees or sum overlapping ownership. Ownership revision alone is not a capture reward; even same-owner writes currently increment it. Event origin/provenance must be server-authored, never a client-supplied trusted flag.

Before active-match disconnect, settle to removal then discard the ruler's balance/remainder/count/timestamp along with the existing runtime ruler lifecycle. Reconnect follows current behavior: fresh Lord, zero Prestige and whatever normal starting allocation is available; no offline earning or restoration. Stable `Player_<UserId>` IDs must not reuse the removed lifetime's timestamp. After match end, existing hierarchy intentionally preserves final rulers/ownership on disconnect; preserve their frozen Prestige too. Late joins remain spectators, with no earning or newly restored title state. No persistence/reconnect redesign is proposed.

At victory, settle all balances exactly to `MatchState.GetFinal().endedAt` using the final pre-end intervals and then stop. Do not add time from the next scheduler callback. ### Pacing risks and approval decisions

Linear earning rewards early expansion and may widen the leader's advantage. A fast conquest-to-Domination match can end before Emperor—or before Duke—because victory is independent of titles. Conversely, a liege with one personal province may wait 25–50 minutes despite ample subject land; that is the deliberate cost of personal earning, and could make political play feel slow. Do not “fix” it with hidden subject income, capture bonuses, rank multipliers, victory delays or invented mechanics. Start at **10/min/province**, then tune from human match evidence; all times scale inversely with rate except land-gated milestones. No inactivity detection: a connected ruler sustaining ownership earns even without recent input.

Planned personal Gold rank bonuses **Duke +0.5%, King +1%, Emperor +2%** do not multiply Prestige and remain unimplemented. They may indirectly accelerate recruitment → personal expansion → Prestige. At the current 10 Gold/min per province, the additions are only 0.05/0.10/0.20 Gold/min per personally owned province; future Gold fractional accounting must retain those amounts rather than truncate them away. No stacking rule is inferred here.

Approve before implementation: **(1)** this time-based personal rule at zero start/10 per province per minute, no decay/spending/cap; **(2)** strict administrative-holding/forced-result exclusion with trusted provenance; **(3)** current disconnect wipe/reconnect reset and exact match-end settlement. Existing cumulative thresholds, subtree land gates and sequential manual establishment are preserved, not changes requiring renewed acceptance. If political progression should routinely precede Domination despite few personal holdings, that pacing goal needs a separate Director decision; it is not resolved by approving this formula alone. Council, authority, permissions, voting and overthrow remain outside scope.
